## S5 Reproduction and corroboration

## S7 Reconstruction coverage

The live traces do not evaluate explicit-Deny policies, arbitrary IAM conditions, external resource-policy grants, permissions boundaries, organization policies or general cross-account behavior. The implementation supports tested explicit-deny cases, but those are outside the live scenario set. Some lifecycle cases, including isolated child use after parent expiry, have only offline validation. These details accompany the restricted evaluator scope in Section 3.3 and the main paper's limitations.
